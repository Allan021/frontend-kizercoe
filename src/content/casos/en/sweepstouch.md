## The client

**Sweepstouch** is a messaging and marketing platform for independent Hispanic supermarkets in the United States. It connects each store with its shoppers through SMS, MMS, RCS and WhatsApp, and today serves 200+ supermarkets and a base of over one million shoppers.

## The challenge

An operation like this has many moving parts, store by store: signing up shoppers in the supermarket, coordinating promoters and cashiers, building and sending campaigns, publishing deals, selling online and measuring what worked. Every step that depended on a person, a spreadsheet or a loose message was a bottleneck that couldn't scale to hundreds of stores.

## The solution

Since 2024 we have led Sweepstouch's technology and moved **every process of the operation into a system**:

- **In-store kiosks:** tablet interfaces so shoppers sign up right there in the supermarket.
- **Promoter and cashier modules:** store staff work from the platform, not from paper.
- **Admin dashboards:** each store and the central team see their operation and results live.
- **SMS, MMS, RCS and WhatsApp campaigns:** tools to create, send and manage them from one place.
- **AI automations:** anything repetitive runs on its own.
- **E-commerce, landing pages and link pages** for each store, tied to the campaigns.
- **Results tracking:** every campaign is measured, from send to purchase.

## The result

**Every process of the operation is now digitized and automated.** From the shopper signing up at the kiosk to the deal reaching their phone, the online purchase and the campaign report, everything runs through the platform. That is what makes it possible to serve 200+ supermarkets without multiplying the team.

## What your business can take from this

1. **If a process depends on one person, it doesn't scale.** The first step is putting it in a system, even a simple one.
2. **Automate what repeats.** Campaigns, notifications and reports don't need someone pressing a button every time.
3. **Measure everything.** Without data on each campaign you don't know what to repeat and what to drop.
