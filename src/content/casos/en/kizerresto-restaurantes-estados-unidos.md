## The client

Restaurants in the United States serving dine-in and takeout. At their request we don't publish their names, but today **several locations run on KizerResto every day**, the restaurant point of sale in the KizerPOS family.

## The challenge

A US restaurant needs a point of sale that keeps up with the rush and charges the way the country requires:

- Orders have to reach the kitchen without paper slips or shouting.
- Tables want to pay separately, each person their own.
- Tips and **Sales Tax** have to add up on every bill and at closing.
- If the internet drops on a Friday night, sales can't stop.

## The solution

We installed **KizerResto** with its terminals and kitchen printers:

- **Tickets straight to the kitchen:** the server takes the order and the kitchen gets it instantly.
- **Bills split per person**, no manual math.
- **Tips and Sales Tax** calculated on every bill.
- **Cash close that matches the drawer**, tips included.
- **Works offline:** each sale is saved on the device and syncs when the connection returns.
- **Support from the team that built it**, in English or Spanish.

## The result

**Several US restaurant locations sell every day with KizerResto.** A system designed and built in El Progreso, Honduras, running in one of the most demanding restaurant markets in the world.

## What it means for your restaurant

The same KizerResto that handles Sales Tax and tips in the US invoices in Honduras with **ISV and CAI**. One system, configured for your country, with terminal and printer installation included.
