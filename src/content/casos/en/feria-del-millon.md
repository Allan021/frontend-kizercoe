## The client

**Feria del Millón** is an art fair in Bogotá, Colombia, focused on discovering emerging artists and connecting their work with new buyers.

## The challenge

An in-person fair sells only during the event, and only to people who walk in. Every buyer who couldn't attend, or who kept thinking about a piece after leaving, was a lost sale for the artist and for the fair.

The fair needed its experience to exist online too: anyone should be able to browse the works, fall for one and buy it on the spot, with a payment method Colombian buyers already use.

## The solution

We designed and built the fair's e-commerce platform:

- **Artwork catalog** with a page for each piece and its artist.
- **Browse by artistic technique**, so buyers find what they want without knowing artists' names.
- **Online checkout with Mercado Pago**, the most widely used payment method in Colombia.
- **Built with Next.js**, fast on mobile, where most of the social-media traffic lands.

The whole project was delivered remotely from Honduras, with progress demos and a price agreed in writing.

## The result

**Over US$450,000 in sales through the platform.** The fair no longer depends only on in-person visitors: art can be discovered and bought from anywhere, before, during and after the event.

## What your business can take from this

1. **A physical store has a ceiling; an online store doesn't.** If you only sell to people who walk in, there are customers looking for you who can't find you.
2. **Payment methods matter as much as design.** Charge with what your customer already uses.
3. **Distance is not a problem.** This project was built for Colombia from El Progreso, Honduras.
